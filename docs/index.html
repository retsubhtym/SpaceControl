SpaceControl: extra controls for Mission Control.

SpaceControl is a free, open-source macOS menu-bar app that extends Mission Control with keyboard navigation, window controls, and shortcuts for managing Spaces. Close or minimize windows, navigate thumbnails, and move windows between Spaces from Mission Control.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Free & open source · MIT licensed · tested on macOS 27

Window controls

Everything you need to triage windows in Mission Control without touching the mouse.

Close from anywhere

Every window thumbnail gets an × close button. Or just hover a window and press ⌘W.

Keyboard navigation

Move the selection with ←↑↓→ or hjkl. The cursor — and Mission Control's own hover highlight — follows your selection.

Minimize, hide, quit

⌘M minimizes the selected window, ⌘H hides its app, and ⌘Q quits its app (apps can still prompt to save).

Open & preview

Return opens the selected window. Space previews it as usual — close buttons hide automatically during preview.

Spaces controls

Create, jump to, and move windows between Spaces without leaving Mission Control.

New Space

⌘N creates a new Space on the display under your mouse.

Jump to a Space

⌘1…⌘9, ⌘0 jump straight to Space 1–10 on the display under your mouse and close Mission Control.

Move a window to a Space

⌘← / ⌘→ moves the selected window to the previous or next Space.

Every shortcut above is customizable — menu-bar icon → Settings… (or ⌘,) — which also lets you hide the menu-bar icon entirely.

Requirements & permissions

  • The project targets macOS 14 or later, but has been tested on macOS 27. SpaceControl relies on Mission Control’s undocumented accessibility structure and private APIs, so other versions may not work and future releases may change behavior.
  • SpaceControl needs Accessibility access: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. The app will prompt you and can open this pane for you from its menu.
  • Released builds are signed ad-hoc. If you build from source, macOS re-signs the app on every rebuild, so you may need to re-grant Accessibility access after each local build.
